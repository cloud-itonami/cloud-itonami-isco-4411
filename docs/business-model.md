# Business Model: Independent Library & Archive Support Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-4411`
- ISCO-08: `4411`
- Occupation: Library Clerks
- Social impact: public-information-access, literacy, local-jobs

## Customer

- independent/community libraries
- archives
- reading rooms

## Offer

- circulation management
- collection cataloging
- archival material access
- interlibrary loan coordination

## Revenue

- membership/subscription fee
- archival access fee
- cataloging service fee

## Trust Controls

- no restricted/archival material release without governor gate
- patron borrowing records are confidential and require consent to disclose
- circulation records are auditable, not editable
